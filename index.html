    ✨ Examples

    Questions, answered

    Is it free?

    Yes. Building a kit, editing it and exporting are free. Stamping a plan or ticking a milestone is a normal BOT Chain transaction, so you pay a tiny network fee from your own wallet. We don't charge anything on top.

    Will my drafts make things up?

    No. Drafts only use facts you put in the brief. They never invent numbers, prices, discounts, reviews or awards — anything that needs proof is marked ⚠ Add proof so you can fill in the real thing. Always read your drafts before posting.

    Which channels?

    X, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok (as a video script), Email and Community (Discord or Telegram). Each channel gets a realistic number of posts per week, and launch day gets a hero post on every channel you pick.

    Can I post directly?

    Not yet. Export to the tools you already use: a CSV for schedulers like Buffer or Later, an .ics file with one calendar event per post, a pretty PDF report, or copy every post in one go.

    What goes on BOT Chain?

    Only a fingerprint (a hash) of your plan, its title, goal, launch date and the number of milestones. Your drafts, personas and checklist text stay in your browser. When you tick a milestone, only "milestone #3 is done" is recorded.

    Do I need a wallet?

    Only to stamp a plan and tick milestones. You can build, edit and export a whole kit without one. To stamp, connect MetaMask — we'll switch it to BOT Chain (chain ID 677) for you.
